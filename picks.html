ROCK’n’Roll Wagers

Pick a side. Say your price. Someone takes it.

Play money, settled up after each Monday. The rest is behind the ?.

How the board works

Putting one up

Pick a side and name your own price: what you’ll risk, and what you want to win. Risk $5 to win $10 and you’ve offered 2-1.

Taking one

Each court shows its two teams side by side, and every price sits under the team you’d be backing. Take it and you’re on that team for that amount — all of it or part of it. Whoever put it up is on the other end, and their name is under the price.

Settling

Nothing is held and no balance is checked. A pick is a promise between two people, and Settle Up adds each Monday together and says who owes whom.

Three titles are in play

  • 👑 Wager King — most won over the season.
  • 🎲 Most Action — most money matched, either end of it. A price nobody takes doesn’t count.
  • 📣 Market Maker — most prices you put up that somebody took. The size doesn’t matter, only that they were wanted.

They run all season and are on the standings page.

The rules

  • The board shuts when the first ball goes up.
  • Playing that night? You can only back your own team.
  • $1 to $100 to risk, up to $500 to win, nothing longer than 10-1 either way.
  • Anyone can read the board. You need to be signed in to make a pick.
