2026 WBP Championship Recap: The Cinderella Run

Nobody gave them a chance. At 9-to-1 odds and seeded fifth out of eight teams, Dolan and Napolitano weren't supposed to be here. But on a March night at Wee Burn Country Club — one that ended with a rainbow stretching across the courts — they proved the oddsmakers wrong in the most dramatic fashion imaginable.

The Quarterfinals

The top seed, D'Acunto and McGurren, handled Jonson and Luecke 6-4, 6-2 in a businesslike opener. Baum and Moyers, the defending champions looking to repeat, dispatched Ridder and Dolcetti 6-2, 4-4(8-6). Adams and Brown delivered the most dominant performance of the round, blanking Smith and Walsh 6-2, 6-0.

But the match that set the tone for the entire tournament came on Court 2. Dolan and Napolitano found themselves in a war with the fourth-seeded Grills and Rayhill. After splitting the first two sets 6-4, 3-5, it came down to a 10-point super tiebreaker. Dolan and Napolitano survived 10-8. It was a sign of things to come.

The Semifinals

Riding that momentum, the five seed came out firing against the tournament's top-seeded team, taking the first set 6-2. D'Acunto and McGurren punched back to take the second 6-4. For the second straight match, Dolan and Napolitano were headed to a deciding tiebreaker — and for the second straight match, they gutted it out 10-8. On the other side, Baum and Moyers handled Adams and Brown 6-3, 6-4, setting up a finals showdown between the defending champs and the hottest team in the bracket.

The Finals

What followed was one of the greatest matches in WBP history. Neither team could separate. The first set went to a tiebreaker at 5-5, and Dolan and Napolitano edged it 7-5. The second set? Same script — 5-5, tiebreaker, 7-5 Dolan/Napolitano. Two sets, two tiebreakers, same result. The five seed had done the unthinkable, running through the four, the one, and the two seed to claim the 2026 WBP Championship.

The Consolation Bracket

Grills and Rayhill rebounded from their heartbreaking quarterfinal loss, rolling past Jonson and Luecke 6-2, 6-1. Ridder and Dolcetti edged Smith and Walsh in a tight one, 5-5(7-5), 5-2. In the fifth-place match, Grills and Rayhill showed their resilience again — dropping the first set 2-6 before storming back to take the next two 6-2, 7-4.

Awards Night

The hardware matched the drama. Dolan and Napolitano hoisted the Red Hulk championship trophies — fitting for a team that smashed through every obstacle in their path. The Thor Viking hammer made its appearance, and the Best Homie Ever award found a deserving home. The Commish presided over the ceremony as 16 members gathered for a group photo that capped off one of the best nights in WBP history.

By the Numbers

Dolan and Napolitano played three matches, went to a deciding tiebreaker in two of them, and won every single clutch moment. Three of their four tiebreakers were decided by just two points. At 9-to-1 pre-tournament odds, this is the biggest upset run the league has ever seen.

Sometimes the ball just bounces your way. And sometimes a rainbow shows up to prove it was meant to be. And most importantly, Devo won the first ever Vinny's WBP Award.

Key Storylines

  • D'Acunto / McGurren (#1) earned the top seed with the strongest combined season — D'Acunto leads the field at 67% this year (4-2), and McGurren's 161 career matches make him one of the most battle-tested players in the bracket.
  • Baum / Moyers (#2) are the defending champions looking to go back-to-back. Baum carries a 57% career win rate over 83 matches and is undefeated this season (only playing 1 match!). Moyers is a grizzled DPC player looking for a twopeat. They know what it takes to win it all, and that championship experience is something no other team can match.
  • Smith / Walsh (#3) bring the most combined experience of any team in the field. Smith is rolling at 60% this season — he is the OG of the group with the most experience of anyone. Walsh adds veteran savvy. Seeds don't matter once the ball is in play.
  • Grills / Rayhill (#4) are the hottest duo in the bracket with a perfect 2-0 record as partners this year. Grills' 228 career matches are the most of any player in the tournament. Don't sleep on this team.
  • Dolan / Napolitano (#5) pair Dolan's impressive career win rate with Napolitano's big-game experience. Dolan has a 70% career win rate, impressive! A fresh partnership that could gel at just the right time.
  • Adams / Brown (#6) are the most balanced team in the tournament — steady and dangerous with no glaring weakness. Adams with the Ginsu, Downtown with the best drive of the league. They're a tough out for anyone.
  • Ridder / Dolcetti (#7) feature one of the season's most active players in Ridder, who's played a bracket-high 15 matches at a 60% clip (9-6) with 150 career matches. Dolcetti adds 87 career matches of experience. If they click, watch out.
  • Jonson / Luecke (#8) pack serious experience — Jonson's 225 career matches are the second-most in the tournament, with a 60% season rate (6-4) and 54% career mark. Luecke adds grit to a team that could make noise as an 8-seed.

Bottom line: this is anyone's tournament. Just 5.5 points separate the favorite from the longest shot. Every team has a legitimate path to the title.

Format & Rules
  • Sets: 2 sets, 1-ad scoring, tiebreak at 5-5
  • Third set (non-finals): If the match is tied after 2 sets, a 10-point tiebreaker decides the winner
  • Circuit breaker (first two rounds only):
    • Matches have a 45-minute time limit
    • At the 45-minute mark, if there is a 2-game difference, the set is awarded to the leader
    • If games are even, stop and play a 7-point tiebreaker
    • If there is a 1-game lead (e.g. 4-3), finish the game in progress — if the leader then has a 2-game edge, they win the set; if not, play a 7-point tiebreaker
    • If split sets after the circuit breaker, a 10-point tiebreaker decides the match
  • Finals: Full sets, no circuit breaker
  • Food & Drink: Pizza and wings provided — BYOB
