Key Storylines

  • D'Acunto / McGurren (#1) earned the top seed with the strongest combined season — D'Acunto leads the field at 67% this year (4-2), and McGurren's 161 career matches make him one of the most battle-tested players in the bracket.
  • Baum / Moyers (#2) are the defending champions looking to go back-to-back. Baum carries a 57% career win rate over 83 matches and is undefeated this season (only playing 1 match!). Moyers is a grizzled DPC player looking for a twopeat. They know what it takes to win it all, and that championship experience is something no other team can match.
  • Smith / Walsh (#3) bring the most combined experience of any team in the field. Smith is rolling at 60% this season — he is the OG of the group with the most experience of anyone. Walsh adds veteran savvy. Seeds don't matter once the ball is in play.
  • Grills / Rayhill (#4) are the hottest duo in the bracket with a perfect 2-0 record as partners this year. Grills' 228 career matches are the most of any player in the tournament. Don't sleep on this team.
  • Dolan / Napolitano (#5) pair Dolan's impressive career win rate with Napolitano's big-game experience. Dolan has a 70% career win rate, impressive! A fresh partnership that could gel at just the right time.
  • Adams / Brown (#6) are the most balanced team in the tournament — steady and dangerous with no glaring weakness. Adams with the Ginsu, Downtown with the best drive of the league. They're a tough out for anyone.
  • Ridder / Dolcetti (#7) feature one of the season's most active players in Ridder, who's played a bracket-high 15 matches at a 60% clip (9-6) with 150 career matches. Dolcetti adds 87 career matches of experience. If they click, watch out.
  • Jonson / Luecke (#8) pack serious experience — Jonson's 225 career matches are the second-most in the tournament, with a 60% season rate (6-4) and 54% career mark. Luecke adds grit to a team that could make noise as an 8-seed.

Bottom line: this is anyone's tournament. Just 5.5 points separate the favorite from the longest shot. Every team has a legitimate path to the title.

Format & Rules
  • Sets: 2 sets, 1-ad scoring, tiebreak at 5-5
  • Third set (non-finals): If the match is tied after 2 sets, a 10-point tiebreaker decides the winner
  • Circuit breaker (first two rounds only):
    • Matches have a 45-minute time limit
    • At the 45-minute mark, if there is a 2-game difference, the set is awarded to the leader
    • If games are even, stop and play a 7-point tiebreaker
    • If there is a 1-game lead (e.g. 4-3), finish the game in progress — if the leader then has a 2-game edge, they win the set; if not, play a 7-point tiebreaker
    • If split sets after the circuit breaker, a 10-point tiebreaker decides the match
  • Finals: Full sets, no circuit breaker
  • Food & Drink: Pizza and wings provided — BYOB
